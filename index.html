Repository

The repository is still under active development. You may visit it at /uloong/.

Status

There is still a lot of work ahead:

  • Dart/Flutter is a blocker for the installer and other desktop applications; Xi Ruoyao is working on it.
  • Work continues on covering the remainder of the main and universe repositories.
  • Cluster and standard Juju-driven automation are under construction - OpenStack support must come first, a blueprint is open and we are actively working with upstream maintainers to include LoongArch support.
